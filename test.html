01   /   THE VOLCANIC WORLD
Beneath the surface

How a volcano moves

Three forces shape the story—from molten rock deep below to new ground above.

01 — Source01

Magma rises

Hot molten rock and gas collect below the crust, moving upward through weaknesses in the Earth's surface.

02 — Release02

Pressure breaks

As pressure builds, magma may erupt as flowing lava, ash, and volcanic gases—sometimes gently, sometimes explosively.

03 — Renewal03

Land is remade

Cooled lava and ash add new layers to the landscape, creating volcanic rock and, over time, new landforms.

A landscape in layers
Every eruption is a moment in a much longer story of creation and change.
Look closer

Beautiful, powerful, worth understanding.

Volcanoes are part of Earth's natural systems. Learning about them—and following local monitoring and safety guidance—helps us respect both their wonder and their risks.
